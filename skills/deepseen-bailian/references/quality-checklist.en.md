# Completed skill checklist

Check actual generated files and record pass, pending or not applicable with evidence; an empty form is not a finished skill.

1. **Trigger:** identifiable name, use and non-use boundaries, and the user's actual task preserved.
2. **Inputs:** required and optional materials; reuse answers and ask specific missing facts.
3. **Criteria:** key judgments trace to user confirmation or sources; separate fact, inference, unknown and hypothesis; do not invent weights or thresholds.
4. **Actions:** inputs, branches, outputs and checks are actionable, decision-changing unknowns handled first, and rules do not conflict.
5. **Delivery:** actual complete SKILL.md, readable references, copyable output structure and usage.
6. **Cases:** normal, negative/non-use and missing/boundary inputs with expected outputs; an additional case not used in authoring; synthetic materials labeled.
7. **Dependencies and permissions:** only real tools; authorized saving, network, charges and sending; query the original request when acceptance is unknown.
8. **Evidence level:** distinguish structural checks, simulated runs, real user work and independent review; mark unrun checks pending, not an improvement over a baseline.
9. **Consistency and case evidence:** reconcile rules, output, examples and instructions. A typical user-described scale is not an automatic restriction. Trace each test to its input; do not identify an ambiguous speaker, invent dates or results, or present author self-review as independent.
10. **Defaults and configuration:** mark unspecified statuses and sensitive-data choices unconfirmed; use data minimization without claiming prior user approval. Follow the user's requested format without requiring a permanent skill edit.

Repair ordinary quality issues and retest. If a core business judgment is missing, preserve the draft and ask a specific question. Do not invent facts or claim a hypothesis is user-approved to pass the checklist.
