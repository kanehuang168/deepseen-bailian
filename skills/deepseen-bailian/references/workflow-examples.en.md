These fictional illustrations are not actual search results, tool calls or independent acceptance.

## Find a meeting-minutes skill

User requests a skill separating decisions and discussion and recording owners and deadlines. The route is find: check available skills and search tools; disclose lack of network rather than invent links. If candidate content is actually available, use CREATE to check the meeting scope, input notes/transcript, criteria separating decisions from discussion and marking missing deadlines, steps, output template and missing-material handling. Record source and license.

If Xixing is actually callable, hand it the candidate text, source and goal and record its returned comparison. Otherwise explicitly state it was not invoked and analyze the material read. Deliver candidates, sources, fit and gaps without automatically installing.

## Distill customer-lead judgment

This is fictional, not any company's established standard. User wants a skill for overseas buyer leads and says purchase intent, budget and willingness to talk matter. Ask whether an unasked budget should be clarified and whether visiting is mandatory. User says ask first; visiting is a bonus. Ask for positive and negative examples and whether deferral is permanent. User says follow up on clear needs and willingness to talk; defer a lead with no purchase intention or budget after asking, with later reassessment possible.

Build an actual SKILL.md from confirmed information: distinguish unknown from explicitly absent intent; ask about an unknown budget; treat a visit as a bonus; output follow up, ask or defer with reasoning and next steps. Do not invent industry thresholds. Simulated runs: need known and budget unasked → ask about budget, not reject for no visit; no intent and budget after asking → defer with reassessment possible. A new case not used in authoring is still needed for a separate transfer test; this published example is not independent acceptance.
