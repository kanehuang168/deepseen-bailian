# Positive, negative and boundary examples

All examples are fictional and do not represent the user's business rules. Replace them with authorized task materials or mark new examples fictional.

## Positive: meeting action items

Input: “We decided to try Plan A; Lee will organize the materials, but no deadline was given. Someone suggested trying Plan B later; it is not decided.”

Expected: decision—try Plan A; action—Lee organizes materials, deadline unconfirmed; discussion—Plan B. Attribute the result to the input; do not send messages or assign tasks. This separates decisions from suggestions and does not invent a deadline.

## Negative: false certainty

Unacceptable: “The company decided on Plan B, Lee must finish Friday, and everyone has been notified.” Plan B was not decided; Friday and notification do not occur in the input, and sending was not authorized. Return to extraction and revise.

## Missing input and non-use

Without meeting material, request notes or a transcript rather than invent decisions. If the user only wants a sentence polished, do that directly instead of starting a skill-creation workflow.

## Transfer case

Input: “We decided to stop Plan C; Wang will check the budget, with no deadline; whether to start Plan D will be discussed next time.” Expected: stop Plan C is the decision; Wang checks the budget, deadline unconfirmed; Plan D remains discussion. This published example is a reference, not a never-seen independent acceptance case or evidence of meeting outcomes or independent review.
